Jewellery Care Guidelines

Elegant. Timeless. Handle with care.

  1. Handle Gently: Avoid pulling, dropping, or rough surfaces to prevent breakage.
  2. Avoid Moisture & Chemicals: Keep away from water, perfume, lotions, and cleaning products.
  3. Store with Care: Use a soft pouch or separate compartment for storing.
  4. Wear with Intention: Jewellery goes on last, off first, to prevent accidental damage.
  5. Clean Softly: Gently wipe jewellery with a dry, soft cloth. Avoid abrasive cleaners.
